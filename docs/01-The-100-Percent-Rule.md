# The 100% Rule

**Ask the AI, before it produces anything:**

> "Are you 100% ready to execute the prompt, or do you need more information?"

This forces a cheap validation loop: the model reviews the whole session and admits **any** doubt about the next step before it acts.

**Examples:**
- "Are you 100% ready to write the summer holiday checklist, or do you need more information?"
- "Are you 100% ready to deliver the final response email to the accountant, or do you need more details?"
- "Are you 100% ready to generate the updated Python file, or do you need more information?"

**Ask yourself, while gathering material (the first focus):**

> "Have I gathered ALL the relevant and correct information?"
